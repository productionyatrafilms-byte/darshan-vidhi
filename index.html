Whenever we see Jin Mandir, Shikhar or Dhwaja, we must join our hands, bow our head and say 'Namo Jinanam'

Whenever we see Sadhu-Sadhviji Bhagwant, we must join our hands, bow down and say 'Matthaen Vandami.'

Whenever we meet a person or friend, we must say 'Pranam' or 'Jai Jinendra' by joining our hands.

Whenever a person performs Tapsya, we should inquire about their well-being by asking 'Shata Mein Ho?'

Padharo.
